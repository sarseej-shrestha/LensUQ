# Roadmap

## Completed

0.1 established the inspectable binary full-factorial prototype. 0.2 generalized factors, added finite parameter alternatives, reproducible sampling, multiclass and regression audits, pairwise attribution, optional prefix reuse, structured exports, two more demos, and mathematical tests. See [[Final Project Transition]] and [[LensUQ 0.2]].

## Next research gates

Define an estimand for incomplete or dependent spaces before adding sampled attribution. Study factor rationale metadata and schema fingerprints for reproducibility. Evaluate repeated data and model seeds without conflating their variation with preprocessing. Investigate non-sklearn backends after defining a clone-and-fit contract. See [[Open Problems]] and [[Long-Term Architecture]].
