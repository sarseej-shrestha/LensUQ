# LensUQ 0.2

Version 0.2 is implemented. It extends the 0.1 binary prototype with generic `Choice` factors, `ParamChoice`, sampled universes, multiclass and regression audits, main plus pairwise attribution on complete products, optional prefix caching, CSV/JSON exports, and three offline demonstrations. See [[Final Project Transition]], [[Scope]], and [[Architecture Overview]].

Further research is recorded in [[Open Problems]], [[Backlog]], and [[Long-Term Architecture]].
